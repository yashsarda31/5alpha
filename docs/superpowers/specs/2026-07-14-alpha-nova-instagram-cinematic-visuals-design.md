# Alpha Nova Instagram Cinematic Visuals — Design

**Date:** 2026-07-14
**Status:** Approved visual design, awaiting final spec review

## Objective

Create a cohesive six-image Instagram carousel that attracts ambitious retail
traders, affluent active investors, and finance professionals to Alpha Nova.
The images are standalone marketing assets. No application code, interface, or
deployed asset will be changed.

## Selected Direction

The campaign uses **Nova Intelligence** with restrained **Market Architecture**
elements. A faceted silver Alpha Nova prism is the recurring hero object. It
appears within graphite-black cinematic environments shaped by metallic-gold
light and subtle ice-blue data energy.

The work must feel like a premium technology product film: intelligent,
controlled, credible, and aspirational. It must avoid generic fintech imagery,
crypto symbolism, trading-floor clichés, and uncontrolled neon effects.

## Deliverables

Produce six text-free 4:5 portrait images for an Instagram carousel:

1. **Brand reveal:** the Alpha Nova prism awakening in darkness.
2. **Signal clarity:** chaotic market noise converging into one clear signal.
3. **Institutional intelligence:** the prism within monumental financial data
   architecture.
4. **Defined risk:** opportunity protected by a precise luminous risk boundary.
5. **Connected workflow:** market regime, signal, research, and risk represented
   as one continuous visual system.
6. **Closing hero:** the most iconic prism composition with generous space for a
   call to action.

Each image must work independently while clearly belonging to the same campaign.

## Visual System

- **Style:** hyper-real cinematic 3D with premium product-film polish.
- **Palette:** graphite black, brushed silver, metallic gold, and restrained
  electric ice blue.
- **Materials:** precision-machined metal, smoked glass, polished stone, subtle
  atmospheric particles, and controlled reflective surfaces.
- **Lighting:** sculptural gold key light, restrained ice-blue edge light,
  volumetric atmosphere, deep blacks, and readable silhouettes.
- **Camera:** consistent cinematic product photography, strong depth, low visual
  distortion, and a recurring central or lower-third hero composition.
- **Brand reference:** use the existing Alpha Nova logo artwork only as a shape,
  palette, and material reference for the faceted prism. Do not regenerate the
  existing wordmark inside the images.

## Composition and Copy Space

All master images use a 4:5 portrait composition suitable for the Instagram feed.
The subject must remain legible when viewed on a phone. Each frame reserves clean
negative space for later marketing headlines or calls to action. No generated
text, numbers, UI screenshots, watermarks, borders, or platform chrome appear in
the artwork.

## Campaign Constraints

- Do not depict people, laptops, coins, cryptocurrency tokens, robots, city
  skylines, or literal trading floors.
- Do not imply guaranteed returns, wealth promises, or broker execution.
- Keep charts and financial information abstract rather than presenting fake
  tradable data.
- Avoid broad orange backgrounds that reproduce the current logo artwork
  literally; translate its metallic-gold energy into the darker campaign system.
- Keep ice blue secondary to gold and silver.
- Preserve consistent prism geometry, materials, lighting logic, and cinematic
  finish across all six images.

## Generation and Validation

Use the built-in image-generation workflow with the existing Alpha Nova logo as a
visual reference. Generate each distinct image separately. Save all selected final
assets under a new marketing-only folder in the workspace without overwriting any
existing files.

Validate every output for:

- correct 4:5 portrait framing;
- visual continuity with the other campaign images;
- clean negative space and mobile readability;
- recognizable faceted prism geometry;
- absence of unwanted text, watermarks, fake UI, and prohibited clichés;
- premium material detail, controlled highlights, and intact dark tonal range.

If an image misses the direction, revise one visual variable at a time and
revalidate before accepting it.
