# Alpha Nova “9:14:57” Cinematic Ad Design

## Objective

Create a completely new 30-second vertical HTML advertisement for Alpha Nova that earns attention through curiosity and human tension. The film must not reuse the structure, wording, or feature-by-feature montage of the previous advertisements.

## Audience and Format

- Audience: active Indian-market traders aged 20–40
- Platform: Instagram Reels and YouTube Shorts
- Canvas: 9:16 vertical
- Duration: exactly 30 seconds
- Communication: music, sound design, and on-screen text only
- Delivery: a self-running HTML film with replay, mute, and fullscreen controls

## Creative Concept

The film is titled **9:14:57**. It begins three seconds before the Indian cash market opens. A cinematic, realistic trader sits alone in a dark premium workspace. The first three seconds create an unanswered question: what happens at 9:15?

The market open brings sudden motion and sound, but the trader does not panic. Alpha Nova organizes the decision into four beats: discover, analyse, validate, and size risk. The product is shown through the trader’s decision rather than as a catalogue of separate features.

The emotional progression is:

1. Curiosity
2. Tension
3. Controlled intensity
4. Clarity
5. Confidence

## Storyboard and Exact Copy

### 0–3 seconds — Countdown

- Extreme close-up of a digital market clock.
- Time advances from `09:14:57` to `09:14:59`.
- Macro cuts show the trader’s eye, hand above the keyboard, and dark screens waking up.
- No headline. The clock itself is the hook.
- Sound: room tone, electrical hum, heartbeat-like sub pulse, three restrained clock ticks.

### 3–6 seconds — Market open

- Clock reaches `09:15:00`.
- A short moment of total silence precedes a sharp opening-bell impact.
- Market data, charts, and positioning begin moving across the workstation.
- Text: **THE MARKET IS OPEN.**

### 6–10 seconds — Pressure

- Rapid cinematic cuts: watchlist changes, option-chain movement, price breakout, news flash.
- The trader stays composed while reflections move across the face.
- Text: **EVERY SECOND ASKS A QUESTION.**

### 10–15 seconds — Discover

- The trader opens Alpha Nova.
- A market signal resolves from noisy data into one defined setup.
- Entry, stop, and target appear as one coherent decision card.
- Text: **DISCOVER WHAT MATTERS.**

### 15–20 seconds — Analyse and validate

- The setup expands into chart context, options positioning, and business quality.
- Transitions follow the trader’s eye line and cursor rather than cycling through isolated tools.
- Text appears in two beats: **ANALYSE THE MOVE.** then **VALIDATE THE IDEA.**

### 20–24 seconds — Risk

- The trader enters capital and stop distance.
- Position size resolves before any trade action.
- The market remains fast, but the interface and trader remain controlled.
- Text: **SIZE THE RISK.**

### 24–27 seconds — Thesis

- Camera pulls behind the trader. The complete Alpha Nova workflow fills the central display.
- Market movement continues in peripheral screens.
- Text appears over two beats:
  - **THE EDGE ISN’T KNOWING WHAT HAPPENS NEXT.**
  - **IT’S BEING READY WHEN IT DOES.**

### 27–30 seconds — Brand resolution

- Cut to black.
- Alpha Nova logo resolves with a restrained gold pulse.
- Text:
  - **ALPHA NOVA**
  - **ONE TERMINAL. EVERY EDGE.**
  - **START FREE — ALPHANOVA48.IN**
- Compliance line: **Analytics and educational information only. Not investment advice.**

## Visual Direction

- Use a cinematic, realistic trader rather than silhouettes or generic futuristic avatars.
- Workspace is premium but believable: dark room, practical monitor light, restrained gold reflections, no luxury clichés.
- Human imagery must remain consistent across shots.
- Use original high-resolution visual assets without visible text, logos, watermarks, or malformed screens.
- Composite the real Alpha Nova logo and all product text in HTML for sharpness.
- Use photographic depth, shallow focus, rack-focus transitions, light sweeps, reflections, and controlled handheld energy.
- Market-open scenes may use faster cuts, but text and product decisions must remain legible.
- Avoid excessive glitching, neon cyberpunk styling, trading-floor imagery, coins, rockets, profit celebrations, and guaranteed-return implications.

## HTML Production Design

- Create a new file and do not modify or replace either earlier advertisement.
- Keep a true 30-second master clock with deterministic scene timing.
- Use original cinematic raster assets for the trader and workspace.
- Build clocks, market data, Alpha Nova panels, charts, position sizing, typography, and transitions in HTML/CSS/JavaScript so they remain crisp at Retina resolution.
- Use the official Alpha Nova logo asset already present in `web/public`.
- Include generated sound design through Web Audio so the file remains lightweight and copyright-safe.
- Require one user interaction to start playback so browser audio policies are respected.
- Support replay, pause, mute, keyboard control, and fullscreen.
- Respect `prefers-reduced-motion` without changing the story order.

## Quality Gates

- The first three seconds create curiosity without explanatory copy.
- The ad reads as a human decision story, not a product feature slideshow.
- No copy from the previous film appears before the final approved brand line.
- The trader and workstation remain realistic and visually consistent.
- All key text is readable on a 360×640 viewport.
- The HTML maintains a 9:16 canvas without horizontal overflow.
- The timer reaches exactly 30 seconds and supports replay.
- No console errors, missing assets, or external runtime dependencies.
- The production web build succeeds with the new public asset included.
