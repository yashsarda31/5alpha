# Alpha Nova 30-Second Launch Ad Design

## Objective

Create a premium 30-second vertical launch advertisement for Alpha Nova aimed at active traders aged 20–40. The ad must make the product feel fast, focused, and sophisticated while communicating that several research workflows live inside one terminal.

## Format

- Platform: Instagram Reels and YouTube Shorts
- Aspect ratio: 9:16 vertical
- Duration: exactly 30 seconds
- Delivery target: 4K, 30 fps
- Communication: music, sound design, and on-screen text only; no voiceover

## Creative Direction

The selected concept is **One Terminal. Every Edge.** The film begins with fragmented market information, introduces Alpha Nova as the organizing system, moves rapidly through its principal tools, and ends with every capability resolving into one coherent terminal.

The tone is minimal, cinematic, precise, and premium. The visual language uses deep black space, graphite glass, restrained metallic-gold accents, white typography, controlled reflections, and smooth dimensional camera movement. The product interface remains the hero.

## Storyboard and Timing

### 0–3 seconds — Market velocity

- Begin in total darkness.
- A thin gold pulse travels vertically through the frame.
- Brief fragments of candlesticks and market numbers emerge from black.
- On-screen text: **THE MARKET MOVES FAST.**
- Audio: isolated low-frequency pulse, faint data ticks, restrained rising tone.

### 3–7 seconds — Information overload

- Accelerate through fragmented charts, options data, market signals, and research panels.
- Keep imagery elegant and legible rather than chaotic or glitch-heavy.
- Panels move independently in dimensional black space before converging.
- On-screen text: **YOUR RESEARCH SHOULD MOVE FASTER.**
- Audio: pulse gains tempo, crisp ticks and short glass transitions join the rhythm.

### 7–19 seconds — Product capability sequence

- Reveal the Alpha Nova interface with a clean gold light sweep.
- Show five product moments using real-looking Alpha Nova interface styling:
  1. Market Signals with a high-conviction actionable setup and defined entry, stop, and target.
  2. Quantitative Stock Screener filtering a broad universe into a focused shortlist.
  3. Options Intelligence showing open interest, PCR, max pain, and positioning.
  4. Chart Analyser with a candlestick chart, technical overlays, VCP Rating, and Alpha Score.
  5. Fundamentals with concise operating performance and valuation context.
- Each feature receives roughly two seconds and transitions through precise match cuts.
- Short on-screen labels: **SIGNALS. SCREENING. OPTIONS. CHARTS. FUNDAMENTALS.**
- Audio: confident minimal electronic rhythm, precise UI clicks, chart sweeps, and soft impacts synchronized to transitions.

### 19–25 seconds — One workflow

- Pull the camera backward as the separate tools align into one complete Alpha Nova terminal.
- A single gold line connects regime, signal, analysis, validation, and risk.
- On-screen text appears sequentially: **SIGNAL. ANALYSE. VALIDATE. MANAGE RISK.**
- Audio reaches its strongest point, then strips back before the end frame.

### 25–30 seconds — Brand resolution

- Interface recedes into black.
- Alpha Nova logo resolves sharply at center with a restrained gold halo.
- Main line: **ALPHA NOVA**
- Brand line: **ONE TERMINAL. EVERY EDGE.**
- CTA: **OPEN THE FREE TERMINAL**
- URL: **ALPHANOVA48.IN**
- Small compliance line: **Analytics and educational information only. Not investment advice.**
- Audio: clean bass impact followed by a short polished sonic logo and silence.

## Visual Rules

- Use the Alpha Nova name and product terminology exactly as written.
- Prioritize realistic product UI over generic financial dashboards.
- Keep text inside mobile-safe margins and readable for at least 1.2 seconds.
- Use modern geometric sans-serif typography with restrained tracking.
- Use white for primary copy and gold only for emphasis, signals, and transition light.
- Preserve true blacks and high contrast; avoid bright blue generic technology imagery.
- Use smooth macro moves, slow parallax, controlled depth of field, and crisp match cuts.
- Avoid excessive motion blur, distorted text, illegible data, flicker, and glitch effects.
- Do not show people, currency piles, coins, rockets, luxury cars, trading floors, or profit celebrations.
- Do not imply guaranteed returns, effortless profits, or investment outcomes.

## Audio Rules

- No spoken dialogue or voiceover.
- Begin with sparse sub-bass and data ticks.
- Build into a minimal, sophisticated electronic pulse during the feature sequence.
- Synchronize UI clicks and transitions precisely to the beat.
- Resolve with one controlled bass impact and a short distinctive sonic logo.
- Avoid aggressive EDM drops, corporate stock music, and alarm-like sounds.

## Success Criteria

- A viewer understands within 10 seconds that Alpha Nova is a market research terminal.
- At least four major product capabilities are recognizable without pausing.
- The end frame remains readable long enough to retain the brand, proposition, URL, and CTA.
- The film feels premium and restrained rather than promotional or sensational.
- The generated output is suitable for cropping-free playback in a 9:16 mobile feed.
